*Subjects: O – obligatory / E – elective
** Students earn credits in semester 2.
***Students earn credits in semester 6.
****Students indicate one module as obligatory in the course of their further studies. The list of courses that
constitute the module is permanent.

Additional information:
1. Foreign students are required to complete a 60-hour course of Polish which ends with an
examination at the A1 level. Students earn 5 credits when they have passed the examination. Polish
students take up an elective course instead.
2. All students are required to complete a 180-hour course of a foreign language other than English and
Polish. A2 is the minimum requirement as to the level at which the course must be completed. The course
starts in semester 3. Students earn 12 credits when they have passed the final examination. Students
must earn credits in semester 6 at the latest.
