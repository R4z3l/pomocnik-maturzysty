Opis kierunku

Data and Society is an inter­di­sci­pli­nary bache­lor’s pro­gram that explo­res the com­plex rela­tion­ships between data, tech­no­logy, and con­tem­po­rary social chal­len­ges. Com­bi­ning socio­logy, data science, sta­ti­stics, pro­gram­ming, and cri­ti­cal social ana­ly­sis, the pro­gram pre­pa­res stu­dents to under­stand, ana­lyse, and inter­pret data in its full social, eco­no­mic, and poli­ti­cal con­text.

The cur­ri­cu­lum focu­ses on three major areas: work and the digi­tal eco­nomy, urban deve­lop­ment and smart cities, and migra­tion and mobi­lity. Stu­dents learn how data shapes labour mar­kets, urban life, gover­nance, ine­qu­ality, and social change. Spe­cial empha­sis is placed on ethi­cal reflec­tion, cri­ti­cal thin­king, and respon­si­ble data use.

Deli­ve­red enti­rely in English, the pro­gram equips stu­dents with strong ana­ly­ti­cal and rese­arch skills, pre­pa­ring them for inter­na­tio­nal care­ers in social data ana­ly­sis, public policy, rese­arch, and socially respon­si­ble busi­ness.

Why choose us?

What makes Data and Society unique?

Rare inter­di­sci­pli­nary com­bi­na­tion of socio­logy, data science, and cri­ti­cal tech­no­logy stu­dies.

Human-cen­tred appro­ach to data – no tech­no­cra­tic or purely com­pu­ta­tio­nal per­spec­tive.

Strong ethi­cal and social focus, addres­sing ine­qu­ality, surve­il­lance, and digi­tal power.

Fle­xi­ble study paths with the­ma­tic blocks: work, city, migra­tion, data.

Rese­arch-based teaching, rooted in cut­ting-edge socio­lo­gi­cal rese­arch.

Full English-taught pro­gram, pre­pa­ring for global care­ers.

This pro­gram offers a rare oppor­tu­nity to study data in its real social con­text, pre­pa­ring gra­du­ates for com­plex ana­ly­ti­cal and stra­te­gic roles.

What you’ll learn
Stu­dents deve­lop advan­ced com­pe­ten­ces in:

social data ana­ly­sis and inter­pre­ta­tion

sta­ti­stics and Big Data in social scien­ces

basics of pro­gram­ming for social rese­arch

data visu­ali­za­tion and sto­ry­tel­ling

socio­logy of work, migra­tion, and urban stu­dies

cri­ti­cal stu­dies of data­fi­ca­tion and AI

public policy ana­ly­sis

rese­arch design and metho­do­logy

ethi­cal and respon­si­ble data use

Dla kogo?

Dla kogo?

This pro­gram is ideal for stu­dents who:

are inte­re­sted in data ana­ly­sis com­bi­ned with social scien­ces

want to under­stand how tech­no­logy influ­en­ces society, cities, work, and migra­tion,

seek care­ers in policy ana­ly­sis, rese­arch, NGOs, and inter­na­tio­nal orga­ni­za­tions

value cri­ti­cal thin­king, ethics, and social respon­si­bi­lity, want to work in inter­na­tio­nal and inter­di­sci­pli­nary envi­ron­ments.

Co po studiach?

Gra­du­ates are pre­pa­red for ana­ly­ti­cal, rese­arch, and stra­te­gic roles such as:

  • social data ana­lyst
  • public policy ana­lyst
  • rese­arch assi­stant
  • urban and migra­tion policy spe­cia­list
  • ESG and CSR ana­lyst
  • HR ana­ly­tics spe­cia­list
  • NGO pro­ject coor­di­na­tor
  • social inno­va­tion con­sul­tant
  • junior rese­ar­cher in think tanks and rese­arch insti­tu­tes
