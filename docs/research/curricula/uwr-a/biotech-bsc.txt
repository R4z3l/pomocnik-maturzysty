At BSc Biotechnology studies you will:
acquire the knowledge required for understanding of biological structures and processes,
develop the skills in application of various experimental techniques in the area of molecular biology and biochemistry,
learn methods and tools used for collection, analysis and presentation of data during professional work,
become prepared to conduct independent or collaborative research projects,
have the opportunity to participate in international exchange programs, such as Erasmus.
The program lasts 3 years (6 semesters). Biotechnology students have access to modern, well-equipped laboratories. Our lecturers are experts in the field of biotechnology and have extensive experience in both scientific research and industrial practice. The faculty’s research activities are closely linked to the teaching program and the best students are given the opportunity to actively participate in research projects. To develop their interests, students can join student science clubs. The graduate of the first-cycle studies in biotechnology has advanced knowledge in biochemistry, molecular and cell biology and related areas, is able to apply this knowledge in biotechnology, is prepared to work in the laboratory, design scientific experiments, apply appropriate experimental methods and analyse the results. The graduates can choose from a wide range of career opportunities: research and development, pharmaceutical, food and agricultural industries, healthcare, clinical diagnostics, clinical research, environmental protection, biomedical engineering, scientific services and equipment supply. The degree also enables graduates to continue their education (master studies in Biotechnology, Molecular Biology, Microbiology, Biochemistry etc).

(on-line interview) Admissions are conducted on a rolling basis.
The exam is conducted by the admissions committee as an online interview via MS_Teams. The interview date is selected by candidates through the IRC system (after all required documents have been submitted and verified).
The internal exam is scored on a scale of 0–20 points, with a minimum of 10 points required to pass. The interview result is entered into the system within 24 hours of the interview. Candidates who receive a positive decision will receive a Letter of Offer within the following 24 hours.
Scope of material:
– cell structure (structure and functions of cell organelles in eukaryotes; comparison between eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells);
– structure and biological function of biomolecules (sugars, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids);
– basic processes of genetic information transmission (replication, transcription, and translation; genetic code);
– basics of human physiology (blood composition and its role in the human body, human immune system, digestive enzymes);
– main metabolic pathways of the cell (aerobic respiration, fermentation, photosynthesis, glycolysis, citric acid cycle, respiratory chain);
– biotechnology – examples of applications in the pharmaceutical industry and agriculture;
– definitions of basic chemical concepts (Avogadro’s number, mole, molar quantity, mass, weight, viscosity, density, osmosis, diffusion, mass number, isotopes, pH).
Literature: Michael L. Cain, Lisa A. Urry, Steven A. Wasserman: Campbell Biology.
