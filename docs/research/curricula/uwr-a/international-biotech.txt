Why Choose Biotechnology?
Hands-On Learning
: You won’t just be studying theories – you’ll be applying them in real-world projects, gaining experience that will set you apart in the job market. In our Biotechnology program, the focus is on putting the knowledge into practice. You’ll spend a significant portion of your time working in a laboratory, conducting experiments, analyzing results, and preparing reports. This hands-on approach allows you to gain practical experience in laboratory methods and build self-confidence.
Industry Connections
: Thanks to collaboration with researches, meetings with professionals and experts, you will gain valuable insights and networking opportunities that can open doors to your future career.
Modern Curriculum
: Take advantage of the program that provides the solid background at the same time demonstrating the latest trends, technologies, and practices in the field of biotechnology.
Flexible Pathways
: Tailor your learning experience to your career goals with a variety of electives and internships that give you the freedom to focus on what excites you the most.
What Will You Learn?
Core Knowledge
: By building a strong foundation in key areas of biotechnology, you will become familiar with a variety of research strategies and acquire the ability to independently solve scientific problems.
Practical Skills
: You will  gain excellent methodological skills and obtain hands-on experience in molecular biology and genetic engineering methods, cell culture techniques, bioinformatics allowing you to design and perform experiments in biotechnology laboratories.
Creative Thinking & Problem Solving
: You will learn to think critically and analyze complex problems, you will be prepared to discuss present knowledge and experimental data and work effectively in team.
Professional Development
: Develop essential skills like teamwork, communication, leadership, and project management.
Career Opportunities
Graduates of the Biotechnology program can pursue careers in scientific institutions as well as biotechnological/pharmaceutical companies:
Working as research scientist, industrial/medical/agricultural biotechnologist, project manager, lab technician, clinical medical representative, science editor, teacher or launching their own biotechnological business
Enjoying career flexibility with roles that span across research, development and biotechnological entrepreneurship.

Entrance exams
The entrance exam is mandatory for all candidates, except those holding an IB diploma or the Polish Matura Exam (instead, their qualifications are
evaluated based on final grades in biology and chemistry
from their previous education).
Type of exam:
Online conducted oral exam.
Scope of material:
Cell structure (structure and functions of cell organelles in eukaryotes; comparison between eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells)
Structure and biological function of biomolecules (sugars, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids)
Basic processes of genetic information transmission (replication, transcription, and translation; genetic code)
Basics of human physiology (blood composition and its role in the human body, human immune system, digestive enzymes)
Main metabolic pathways of the cell (aerobic respiration, fermentation, photosynthesis, glycolysis, citric acid cycle, respiratory chain)
Biotechnology – examples of applications in the pharmaceutical industry and agriculture
Definitions of basic chemical concepts (Avogadro’s number, mole, molar quantity, mass, weight, viscosity, density, osmosis, diffusion, mass number, isotopes, pH)
The oral exam will be assessed on a scale of 0–20 points. A minimum of 10 points is required to pass. Admission will be granted to candidates with the highest scores within the established limit of available places.
We encourage to start preparing in advance.
