Genetics and Experimental Biology
! Genetics and Experimental Biology is the first-cycle degree program that provides reliable and in-depth knowledge of biological processes at the cellular and molecular levels. During lectures and laboratories, you will find answers to all your most pressing questions. The emphasis on laboratory work will introduce you to modern techniques in genetic engineering, histology, cytology, and physiology, and will teach you how to operate specialized equipment You will also learn how to use molecular genetics data in biology, biochemistry, as well as in phylogenetic and environmental research.
