Meet Us
We are delighted that you consider Wroclaw 
University of Economics and Business a study 
destination. Operating since the year 1947, our 
University is deeply rooted in the academic 
history of Poland, maintaining the position as the 
premier business college in the dynamic economic 
landscape of the Lower Silesia region. 
Throughout its history, we have helped thousands 
of alumni to develop professional skills in the fields of 
economics, finance, management, marketing, and engi-
neering management. Our modern teaching approach, 
in conjunction with the extensive educational and 
research experience, is certain to offer you an unparal-
leled opportunity to rapidly acquire the skills required to 
accomplish your goals. Join our community, develop the 
passion and build the future on your own terms! 
We know how difficult it might be pick the right path. 
This guide will assist you in identifying the most suita-
ble option by illustrating the varied potential of each 
route. Each course is tailored to meet the requirements 
of particular sectors in the contemporary economy. The 
classes are taught through a hands-on approach that 
emphasizes the rational application of knowledge. Being 
a business school, we are acutely aware of the practical 
significance of education. 
We hope that you will desire to reach another level – 
together with us! 
Natalia a graduate in Business Management

BUSINESS INFORMATICS
Full-time Studies
Business Informatics is a full-time programme combining advanced IT 
knowledge with management and business competencies. It provides solid 
theoretical foundations and practical skills to design, analyse and imple-
ment IT solutions supporting digital organisations and processes. 
The modular structure allows students to shape their educational path 
according to individual interests and career goals. In the final semesters, 
they select two complementary modules forming a coherent compe-
tence profile, integrating technical, analytical and managerial perspectives                
in response to the evolving digital economy. 
Courses use modern technologies, including AI solutions, no-code/low-
-code platforms, data visualisation and contemporary development 
environments. Cooperation with industry practitioners through guest  lec-
tures and applied projects ensures alignment with labour market needs.

FINANCE
Full-time Studies
The Finance programme is a full-time Bachelor’s degree designed          
alignment with global financial standards. This programme is tailored 
for students eager to explore the dynamic world of finance. The pro-
gramme is delivered entirely in English by a diverse team of Polish and 
international professors and lecturers. 
In addition to foundational subjects such as economics, management,  
law, mathematics, statistics, and digital technologies, the curriculum delves 
into specialized areas including corporate finance, banking, insurance, 
financial markets, real estate, financial reporting, and risk analysis.
Students also acquire practical skills, equipping them for careers in enter-
prises and financial markets. The programme’s advanced level ensures 
that students can easily secure internships during their studies and find 
employment upon graduation.

PRIORITIZE YOUR 
WELLBEING AT WUEB
Studying abroad is an exciting adventure, but adapting to a new country 
can sometimes feel overwhelming. At Wrocław University of Economics 
and Business (WUEB), your mental health, physical health, and comfort 
are our top priorities. We offer a full ecosystem of free support systems 
designed specifically to help you thrive.
 o WUEB Wellbeing Initiatives                                                                                   
WUEB promotes student wellbeing through initiatives that 
encourage students to take care of their physical and mental 
health.
 o WUEB Wellbeing Day                                                          
Take part in lectures, workshops and activities focused on physical 
and mental health, wellbeing and a healthy lifestyle. The event 
brings together students, university staff and experts to explore 
practical ways of supporting wellbeing during university life.
 o Take Care of Your Wellbeing                                                                                            
Explore the wellbeing activities and support available at UEW 
throughout the academic year. Join workshops, take part in 
wellbeing events and discover practical ways to support your health 
and wellbeing while studying.
