Are you ready to navigate the world of international business? Our programme equips you with the knowledge, skills, and hands-on experience needed to understand global markets, international trade, finance, and multinational enterprises.
Through real-world case studies, interactive projects, and discussions with international business experts, you will explore the dynamics of global markets. You will gain insights into key economic, managerial, cultural, political, and social factors shaping international business today.
This programme goes beyond theory. You will develop critical and creative problem-solving skills through hands-on projects, business simulations, and case studies. Taught by experienced Polish and international lecturers, as well as industry professionals, the courses provide a truly global perspective and networking opportunities that will prepare you for a dynamic career in international business.
Graduates of this programme are well-prepared for diverse careers in international trade, finance, consulting, logistics, and multinational corporations. You will also gain the entrepreneurial mindset and skills needed to start your own international business or expand a family enterprise into global markets. For those looking to further their education, this degree provides a strong foundation for pursuing a Master’s degree at top institutions worldwide.
The International Business programme holds the prestigious EFMD Accredited label, a mark of excellence that guarantees high academic standards and strong industry relevance.

The key attributes expected of the students on graduation include:
take advantage of cross-cultural knowledge, communication and language skills to operate effectively within diversified domestic and multinational organisations;
understand the economic, political, legal, social, and cultural forces that affect businesses operating in a globalised economy;
develop the awareness of business realities and the confidence to react flexibly to changes and challenges faced in the global business environment;
have the potential to be entrepreneurial and take leadership roles in their chosen occupations, careers and communities;
communicate in business English effectively and confidently.
