MEDICINE
6-YEAR-LONG UNIFORM STUDIES
General information
Medical studies take 6 years to complete and allow you to gain general knowledge in most fields of medicine. The first two years focus on teaching theory and are to some extent similar to high school in terms of the subjects taught. Clinical classes, i.e. those requiring contact with patients, start in the third year and are the first opportunity to see what a doctor’s work looks like in practice. The last, sixth year of studies is almost entirely dedicated to working with patients in the hospital. After completing studies, the graduates can pass a state exam LEK and complete 13-month-long postgraduate internship in order to gain medical license in Poland, or continue their medical career in other countries.

Why Wroclaw Medical University?
Wrocław Medical University has been recognized as the top Polish university in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings (THE WUR) 2026. We also consistently place in the Shanghai Ranking, CWTS Leiden Ranking, CWUR, Polish universities ranking Perspektywy, etc. Our students not only study under accomplished, experienced faculty, but also have the chance to take part in multiple scientific projects, international conferences, internships and programs with our partner universities.
Classes and subjects
During the first two years, there are more laboratory and theoretical classes  – you work with a microscope, pipette and various types of specimens. There are, of course, lectures and seminars (for which papers are prepared), but classes consisting mainly of listening to the lecturer are rare. From the third year onwards, clinical subjects that require patient contact start to dominate the curriculum. These include, in particular, interview, physical examination, history taking, on the basis of which we learn to make first diagnoses.
Among the taught subjects, anatomy is the one to which most time and attention is devoted at the beginning of studies. Biochemistry is also considered very important. During pathomorphology, in addition to the evaluation of histological specimen, you also participate in autopsies. Other than typical medical subjects there is also medical law, where regulations are discussed on a case by case basis. Later on, you start to learn more about each branch of medicine in different clinics.
Summer internships
Internships take place in a hospital after each year of study (up to and including the fifth year) and last one month. Internship placement can be sought at any hospital, but most students choose places provided by the university under agreements with individual hospital departments. After the first year of study, there is a nursing internship where you learn the basics of patient care. The second year of study is followed by internships in family medicine (3 weeks) and emergency care in a Hospital Emergency Department or ambulance (1 week). After the 3rd year of study you spend a month at the department of internal medicine, after the fourth year you spend two weeks in surgery and another two in anaesthesiology and intensive care. Internships after the fifth year of study take place in the paediatric and gynaecology/obstetrics departments and also last two weeks each.
Study length
The studies take 6 years to complete.
Tuition fee
Tuition fee for Medicine for the year 2026/27 amounts to
67 900 PLN
. You can pay the tuition fee in one, two, or four instalments. In case you decide to pay in two or four instalments, the overall fee will increase accordingly by 2.5% or 3.5%. Conditionally accepted candidates should select the preferred payment option and transfer the fee before the deadline stated in the admission schedule.
Limits and number of points required for admission
For the academic year 2026/27, there are 140 places for students of Medicine in English.
The maximum number of points is 300. The last person admitted to the Medical program in the academic year 2025/2026 achieved a score of 105 points.
Information for students with disabilities
The office for students with disabilities has been established in order to support students and candidates needing any kind of assistance. Candidates applying for the first year of studies who require any help during the application process should specify their needs and notify either the office for students with disabilities or the admission office. Please note that the request for support should be submitted in advance (at least 6 weeks before the deadlines specified in the admission schedule) so that the university can provide adequate assistance. All candidates need to complete the admission process in accordance with the admission requirements.
You can find contact information to the office for students with disabilities here: https://www.umw.edu.pl/pl/kierunek-lekarski/kontakt-niepelnosprawne
