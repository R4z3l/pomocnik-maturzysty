DENTISTRY

5-YEAR-LONG UNIFORM STUDIES

  • General information

    Dentistry is a branch of medicine, which is why studying dentistry is similar to that of medicine in the first years. Dental surgeon deals with prevention and treatment of oral diseases and disorders. After receiving a diploma, he or she can work as a dental surgeon in the field of dentistry as a whole, or he/she can work as a specialist with broader and more specialised knowledge in, for example, conservative dentistry, dental surgery, orthodontics, prosthetics or periodontology.

  • Who should study Dentistry?

    We asked some of our students to whom would they recommend studying dentistry:

    In my opinion, this profession is best described by the unfolding of the word DENTIST. D – doctor (dental surgeon), as the dentist treats not only the teeth, but the whole oral cavity, and should have general medical knowledge. EN – engineer, as the vast majority of treatment is carried out with medical instruments and equipment. And last but not least – artIST, as a dentist should have a sense of aesthetics and skilful hands. People who demonstrate these qualities are best candidates for this profession. Lovers of technological innovations and gadgets will enjoy dentistry, as it is one of the most rapidly developing branches of medicine in terms of technology.

    Elżbieta Midura

    I recommend dentistry to all ambitious people who do not want to have problems finding a place on the labour market and at the same time would like to help many people.

    Michał Perszke

    Anyone who wants to help with oral diseases and disorders, to improve the aesthetics of the teeth and face and to improve the patient’s self-esteem.

    Witold Świenc

As a dentist, I don’t have to simply follow another person’s instructions, which will not always be in line with my vision.

  • Classes and subjects

    Classes take the form of lectures, seminars and practicals. In the first year you will practise hand dexterity, e.g. modelling teeth in wax. On the other hand, pre-clinical classes will begin. You will get ready to work with patients on special phantoms under the watchful eye of dental surgeons. You will meet the patient as early as your third year in clinical subjects such as ophthalmology, internal medicine, neurology, anaesthesiology or surgery.

    In this field of study, you will acquire general medical knowledge (many subjects overlap with medicine, especially in the first years) as well as specialist knowledge. You will study, among other things, anatomy, biophysics, pharmacology, histology, embryology physiology, pathomorphology, pathophysiology with emphasis on dental sections.

    It is important to remember that dentistry is a speciality in which we will encounter more than just one type of patient, taking geriatrics or paediatrics as examples, so a good dentist should be flexible and have a good approach to each patient.

  • Summer internships

    Future dental surgeons are required to complete an internship after each year of study. In the first year these are one-month nursing internships in a hospital, later in dental practices where you can already assist with procedures, and after the third year you can already perform minor procedures yourself.

  • Study length

    The studies take 5 years to complete.

  • Tuition fee

    Tuition fee for Dentistry for year 2026/27 amounts to 78 700 PLN. You can pay the tuition fee in one, two, or four instalments. In case you decide to pay in two or four instalments, the overall fee will increase accordingly by 2.5% or 3.5%. Conditionally accepted candidates should select the preferred payment option and transfer the fee before the deadline stated in the admission schedule.

  • Employment prospects

    Dentists do not have to limit themselves to working in the dental office. You can work in non-public institutions, public institutions or research and development institutes, and you can also fulfil yourself as a teacher, for example at our Medical University.

  • Limits and number of points required for admission

    For the academic year 2026/27, there are 24 places for students of Dentistry in English.

    The maximum number of points is 300. The last person admitted to the Dentistry program in the academic year 2025/2026 achieved a score of 145 points.

  • Information for students with disabilities

    The office for students with disabilities has been established in order to support students and candidates needing any kind of assistance. Candidates applying for the first year of studies who require any help during the application process should specify their needs and notify either the office for students with disabilities or the admission office. Please note that the request for support should be submitted in advance (at least 6 weeks before the deadlines specified in the admission schedule) so that the university can provide adequate assistance. All candidates need to complete the admission process in accordance with the admission requirements.

    You can find contact information to the office for students with disabilities here: https://www.umw.edu.pl/pl/kierunek-lekarski/kontakt-niepelnosprawne
